## Open attestation gaps

These must be checked against one `ANLZ0000.DAT/.EXT/.2EX` set that the owner's
own rekordbox produced, before this branch merges. Nothing in the repository
can stand in for that check, and a third-party implementation is not a
substitute for it.

1. `PMAI` 16-byte trailer: `00 00 00 01 00 01 00 00 00 01 00 00 00 00 00 00`.
2. Scrolling-tag trailer words: `0x00960000`, `0x00960305`, `0` for `PWV4`.
3. Waveform bit layouts, the `PWV6` band scale of 44, the 400/100/1200 column
   counts, the preview floors and the 150 columns/second rate.
4. The 65-entry palette, and the slot-default indices 43/8/23/60.
5. `PCP2` total size 88, byte 29 = 1, and the `memory_count` convention
   (`last index`, `0xffffffff` for hot and empty lists).
6. Section order within each file.
7. `PVBR` length: the writer emitted a 401-word table (1620 bytes) against a
   `.ksy` body of one word plus 400 entries (1616). It now writes 400 entries.
   Confirm one real file is 1616 before relying on this.

Items 1–6 are still worth checking against one real rekordbox file, but they
are no longer a licensing blocker: the owner has waived the gate and the writer
is documented as a translation of `rbl-anlz`.

## Recommendation: 2 PRs, not 4

Recommendation only; nothing is restructured in this PR.

The four-PR split (ANLZ, PDB, job, UI) buys almost nothing and costs a full CI
matrix per step:

- PRs 1 and 2 share every piece of infrastructure: the `PMAI`/section framing,
  the `.ksy`-derived field orders, the CMake entry, the round-trip harness and
  the provenance statement. Splitting them runs the whole writer matrix twice
  and leaves the second half of the framing untested by the first PR's tests.
- PR 3 (job) cannot be meaningfully tested in CI beyond a temp-directory copy
  and a rename; PR 4 (UI) is not exercised by CI at all. Running the matrix for
  a QML action and a worker thread is the most expensive CI in the series for
  the least machine-checkable change.
- Hardware verification is one pass per exported stick regardless of the split,
  so smaller PRs do not reduce the manual work; they multiply the rebuilds.

Proposed shape:

1. **Writers** — ANLZ + PDB, one provenance statement with two evidence tables,
   one round-trip test per format against the in-tree Kaitai readers, one CMake
   change. This is the only part that needs careful review.
2. **Job and UI** — `RekordboxExportJob`, the export dialog target and the QML
   action, with one integration test (export a temp library, re-import it
   through `RekordboxFeature`) and the single hardware check.

If the four-PR shape is kept for review-size reasons, merge at least PR 3 into
PR 4; that is where the CI cost is least justified.
