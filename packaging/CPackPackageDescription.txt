Paramix is a stem-based digital DJ system. Load a track, separate it into
drums, bass, vocals and other, then mix the parts live with per-stem gain,
mute, solo and effects. Wave, Ogg, FLAC and MP3 files can be mixed on a
computer for use in live performances. Filters, crossfader and speed control
are provided. Paramix can sync four decks automatically using an algorithm to
detect the beat.

Paramix works with ALSA or JACK, can be controlled from the GUI or from external
controllers, including MIDI devices and joysticks, and supports skins.
